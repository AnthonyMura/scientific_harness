Quantum Coherence in
Sparse Media

A. Researcher, B. Colleague
Department of Physics · University
We study the persistence of coherence in media whose scattering centers are sparsely distributed.
The local density varies over many length scales, and the decay of the prepared state is governed by a hierarchy of correlation times.
Our main result bounds the decoherence rate in terms of the second moment of the disorder distribution.
